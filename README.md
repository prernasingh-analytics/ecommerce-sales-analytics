## Project Overview

An interactive e-commerce analytics dashboard developed using the publicly available **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset**.

The project explores historical e-commerce transactions from 2016–2018 to understand sales performance, customer orders, product category contributions, and geographic trends.

The dashboard enables users to interactively explore business metrics through filters, KPI cards, and visualizations.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall order volume and delivered item revenue.
- Identify high-performing product categories.
- Explore geographic differences in customer activity and sales.
- Examine sales trends over time.
- Present business performance through an accessible, interactive dashboard.

## Key Insights

Based on the historical Olist dataset:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Total Orders | 99,441 |
| Delivered Orders | 96,478 |
| Delivered Order-Item Records | 110,197 |
| Delivered Item Revenue | R$13,221,498.11 |
| Top Product Category | Health & Beauty |

*Revenue represents the total item price for delivered orders, excluding freight charges. It does not represent net revenue or profit.*

## Dashboard Features

- Interactive KPI cards displaying key business metrics.
- Sales and order analysis through visual charts.
- Product category performance comparisons.
- Customer geography analysis.
- Filters for customer state and product category.
- Browser-based dashboard accessible without installing additional software.

1. Open the live dashboard using the link above.
2. Review the key performance indicators.

5. Compare sales patterns and product category performance.

The dashboard can also be opened locally by downloading `dashboard/index.html` and opening it in a web browser.

## Dataset

**Source:** [Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)

The dataset contains historical Brazilian e-commerce transactions. The analysis is descriptive and does not represent live business performance.

## Limitations

- The dataset is historical rather than real-time.
- Delivered item revenue excludes shipping charges.
- One order may contain multiple products or categories.
- Historical patterns do not necessarily predict future business performance.

## Project Outcome

Developed and published an interactive e-commerce dashboard that makes historical sales and customer-order metrics easier to explore, compare, and interpret.
